Camelia Vasile

Founder & Marketing Strategist

Zoom Direct Media

Technology & Digital / Professional Services

Overview

Founder of Zoom Direct Media, Camelia helps businesses uncover hidden revenue and turn fragmented marketing into predictable client acquisition systems.

Background

Camelia Vasile is the Founder of Zoom Direct Media, a consultancy specialising in funnel strategy, conversion optimisation, and marketing automation. With over 20 years of experience across Europe and Australia, she has worked with businesses in health, HR, engineering, and professional services to deliver measurable revenue growth. Her approach focuses on identifying and fixing hidden conversion gaps before scaling marketing efforts. Known for her sales-first methodology, she prioritises revenue outcomes over vanity metrics. Much of her work has been delivered under NDA, reflecting the calibre and discretion of her client base. Her proprietary 'Clarity Scan' process consistently uncovers immediate revenue opportunities without additional traffic or spend.

Interview

Question 1

What problem were you determined to solve?

Most businesses are sitting on revenue they cannot see because they focus on driving more traffic instead of fixing broken conversion paths. I became focused on identifying these gaps first, as improving what already exists often delivers faster and more reliable results. The revenue is usually already there, but the system is not capturing it effectively.

Question 2

What differentiates your approach from other marketing strategists?

I do not start with campaigns or new assets. I begin by analysing where revenue is currently being lost within an existing system. By optimising these gaps first, businesses often see results without increasing spend or rebuilding everything. It is a less common approach, but it consistently produces stronger outcomes.

Question 3

What has been your most effective growth strategy?

Fixing conversion before scaling has been the most effective strategy. Many businesses try to scale a system that is already underperforming, which only amplifies inefficiencies. By improving conversion rates first, scaling becomes more predictable, cost-effective, and sustainable.

Question 4

What advice would you give to emerging business owners?

Do not chase visibility before fixing your conversion system. More attention will not solve underlying issues and can actually increase costs without improving results. Focus on building a clear, effective path from discovery to conversion, and ensure every step supports revenue generation.
