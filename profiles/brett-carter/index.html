Brett Carter

Owner

Brett Carter Events

Education & Nonprofit / Professional Services

Overview

With over 15 years of experience, Brett Carter is a premier event management specialist in New South Wales, connecting thousands of career seekers with industry leaders through large-scale career and education expos.

Background

With over 15 years of industry experience, Brett Carter has established himself as a leading figure in the New South Wales event management sector, specifically within the careers and further education space. As the founder of Brett Carter Events, he has built a reputation for excellence by bridging the gap between industry leaders and the next generation of workers. His work is defined by a commitment to stakeholder engagement and long-term partnerships, collaborating with major entities such as TAFE NSW, the Australian Defence Force, and various government departments to deliver high-impact recruitment platforms. Brett's professional portfolio is highlighted by the successful execution of premier events, including the Apprenticeship/Traineeship & Jobs Expos and the Greater Western Sydney Careers Market. These flagship events attract over 20,000 annual visitors and feature hundreds of leading exhibitors, providing a vital environment for career seekers and employers to connect directly. By strategically timing these expos to accommodate families and students, Brett ensures that career conversations happen at the most critical decision-making moments, turning large-scale logistics into personal career opportunities. Beyond the logistics of event production, Brett is a dedicated advocate for regional employment and skills development. His presence across social media and digital platforms reflects a focus on community impact, often partnering with local media outlets to ensure broad accessibility for his initiatives. Through his boutique firm, he continues to address skills shortages and recruitment challenges across NSW, leveraging his deep expertise in talent acquisition and project coordination to foster genuine pathways for thousands of individuals entering the workforce

Interview

Question 1

What inspired you to start this business?

I started this business because I saw a recurring disconnect between young people finishing school and the actual industries crying out for talent. After years in the event space, it became clear that while there's no shortage of ambition, there was a massive gap in meaningful connection. What inspired me was the 'lightbulb moment' you see when a student or career seeker talks directly to someone in a trade or profession they never knew existed. I wanted to create a platform that moved beyond just handing out brochures. My goal was to build high-energy, reputable environments where real conversations happen-helping families navigate those big life decisions together while providing industries and various trades a direct line to their future workforce. Ultimately, I'm driven by the impact of seeing 20,000+ people a year find a clearer path forward. There's nothing more rewarding than knowing our events are the starting point for thousands of successful careers across NSW.

Question 2

What problem were you determined to solve?

The core problem I was determined to solve was the fragmentation of the recruitment and education landscape. For too long, there was a significant "communication gap" between three key groups: industries facing critical skills shortages, educational institutions offering training, and the students or job seekers trying to find their way. The Challenges I Targeted: - The "Information Overload" Barrier: Career seekers were often overwhelmed by digital noise and brochures, making it difficult to find clear, actionable pathways. I wanted to bring everyone into one room to provide clarity through conversation. - Addressing Skills Shortages: Industries like the trades, defence, and healthcare were struggling to reach the right talent at the right time. I saw a need for a massive, centralised platform where these sectors could showcase their value to a captive, motivated audience. - Accessibility for Families: High school students often make their biggest career decisions at the dinner table. I was determined to create events that were accessible to parents and guardians-not just during school hours-ensuring the entire support network was informed. By centralising these connections, I aimed to turn a fragmented, confusing process into a streamlined community event that solves recruitment needs for exhibitors and provides life-changing direction for attendees.

Question 3

What legacy are you building through your work?

The legacy I'm building is centred on sustainable community growth and the long-term prosperity of the New South Wales workforce. For me, it's about more than just successful event dates on a calendar; it's about the thousands of individual "Day Ones" that happen because of those events. A Blueprint for Connection I want my legacy to be the bridge that effectively closed the gap between education and employment. By creating a reliable, high-standard infrastructure for career exploration, I am building a future where: - No talent goes to waste: Every student or job seeker, regardless of their background, has a direct line to industry leaders and mentors. - Local industries thrive: By solving immediate recruitment challenges, we ensure that the trades, emergency services, and local businesses have a steady pipeline of passionate workers to sustain our regional economies. - Informed decision-making: I am fostering a culture where career choices are made based on real-world interaction rather than guesswork, reducing the rate of course dropouts and career dissatisfaction. Strengthening the Social Fabric Ultimately, I want to be remembered for strengthening the social and economic fabric of our communities. When we help a young person find their path, we aren't just filling a job vacancy; we are providing them with purpose and financial security. "My goal is for Brett Carter Events to be synonymous with opportunity. I want to look back in 20 years and see a generation of industry leaders who can point to one of our expos as the moment their professional journey truly began."

Question 4

What differentiates your business from others?

What differentiates Brett Carter Events is the unique combination of massive scale and boutique precision. While many event organisers treat expos as a generic logistics exercise, we approach them as a specialised social service for both industry and community. Here is what sets us apart: 1. Niche Sector Authority We don't just "run events"; we specialise specifically in the further education and recruitment space. With 15+ years of focus in this area, we have a deep understanding of the unique requirements of our sponsors and exhibitors as well as the specific anxieties and needs of school leavers. 2. High-Impact Strategic Reach Our flagship events, like the Apprenticeship/Traineeship & Jobs Expos, are some of the largest in the state, attracting over 20,000 visitors. We have the infrastructure to manage massive crowds without losing the quality of the individual experience, ensuring that every attendee has a meaningful interaction. 3. The "Whole Support Network" Approach Unlike many career fairs that operate strictly during school hours, we prioritise accessibility for parents and guardians. We understand that career decisions are family decisions. By creating events that facilitate these joint conversations, we ensure a higher success rate for the pathways our attendees choose. 4. Deep-Rooted Stakeholder Relationships We aren't just an external vendor; we are a trusted partner to NSW government bodies, local media, and industry leaders. These long-standing relationships allow us to curate a calibre of exhibitors that other organisers simply cannot match, providing our attendees with exclusive access to the best opportunities in the country. "In a world of digital noise, we provide tangible, face-to-face credibility. We don't just fill a hall with booths; we create a high-energy environment where the right talent meets the right opportunity at exactly the right time."

Question 5

What do you want people to feel when they interact with your brand?

When people interact with the Brett Carter Events brand-whether they are a nervous student, a parent, or a major industry exhibitor-I want them to feel a profound sense of clarity and confidence. The process of choosing a career or finding the right talent can be incredibly overwhelming. My goal is for our brand to act as the "signal in the noise." Here is the emotional experience we strive to deliver: 1. Empowered, Not Overwhelmed For attendees, the transition from school to work can feel like standing at a crossroads with too many signs. When they step into one of our expos, I want that anxiety to melt away, replaced by the feeling that "I can do this." They should leave feeling equipped with a plan and inspired by possibilities they hadn't previously considered. 2. A Sense of Belonging I want our events to feel like a community hub. For exhibitors, it's the feeling of being among peers and high-calibre professionals. For visitors, it's the realisation that there is a place for their specific skills and interests in the workforce. We aren't just a corporate entity; we are a facilitator of human potential. 3. Absolute Trust Our brand is built on a "no-surprises" philosophy. I want our partners to feel a sense of relief and reliability when they see my name on an event. They should feel certain that the logistics will be seamless, the audience will be relevant, and the environment will be professional. "At the end of the day, I want people to walk away feeling connected. Whether it's a student finding their first job or an employer finding their next star employee, that feeling of 'finding the right fit' is exactly what we aim to create."

Question 6

What impact do you want your business to have?

The ultimate impact I want Brett Carter Events to have is the measurable reduction of youth unemployment and the closing of the skills gap across New South Wales. I don't just want to host successful events; I want to be a primary catalyst for regional economic growth and individual career fulfillment. Here is the specific impact we are working toward: 1. Transforming "What If" into "What's Next" For thousands of young people, the future is a giant question mark. I want our business to be the place where that question gets answered. The impact we strive for is that moment of realisation where a career path becomes a tangible reality. If we can help one student avoid years of career indecision and put them on a path they love, that is a success. Multiply that by 20,000 attendees a year, and we are changing the trajectory of an entire generation. 2. Sustaining Vital Industries Our impact is also measured by the strength of our partners. By providing industries like construction, defence, healthcare, and the trades with a direct pipeline of motivated talent, we are helping to ensure these essential sectors remain vibrant and staffed. We want to be the reason a local business finds the employee who eventually takes over the company, or the reason a government agency meets its recruitment targets with high-quality candidates. 3. Strengthening Local Communities Events like the Greater Western Sydney Careers Market and our regional expos serve as more than just recruitment fairs-they are community landmarks. The impact I want to leave is a stronger, more connected social fabric where local families feel supported and local economies thrive because their workforce is homegrown and highly skilled. "I want the legacy of my business to be measured in careers launched and communities strengthened. If we can continue to be the bridge that connects ambition with opportunity, then we've made the impact I set out to achieve."
