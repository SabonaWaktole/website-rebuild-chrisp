Kirri Garvey

Owner & Professional Photographer

Captured by Kirri

Professional Services

Overview

I help women over 40 feel confident being the face of their business through personal branding photography while capturing meaningful family moments that last a lifetime.

Background

With over 20 years of experience as a professional photographer, I have worked across a wide range of genres before focusing on personal branding and family photography. My passion lies in helping women over 40 feel confident, beautiful and proud to be seen in photographs. I recognised a gap in posing guidance tailored for real women, which inspired me to develop techniques that are practical and empowering. Through my work, I guide clients step-by-step so they not only look great but understand how to feel comfortable in front of the camera. My family photography sessions are designed to be relaxed and connection-focused, allowing genuine moments to unfold naturally. Ultimately, my mission is to ensure women feel worthy of being present in both their business branding and their family's visual history.

Interview

Question 1

What legacy are you building through your work?

I am working to change the way women over 40 see themselves in photographs. Too many women step out of the frame because they don't feel worthy, and I want to shift that belief. My goal is to help them understand they are still beautiful and deserving of being seen. If I can help women confidently exist in their family's visual history, then I know I've made a meaningful impact.

Question 2

What differentiates your business from others?

My approach focuses on the experience first, not just the final images. I ensure every client feels valued, supported and never rushed, creating a safe space where they can relax. I also go beyond simply directing poses by teaching clients how and why certain movements work. This empowers them with confidence they carry into future photos and everyday life.

Question 3

What do you want people to feel when they interact with your brand?

I want every client to feel loved, guided and truly beautiful from the very first interaction. My process is built on kindness, patience and genuine care so they feel safe and understood. I also create a fun and relaxed environment filled with laughter, allowing natural moments to shine. Ultimately, I want them to walk away feeling more confident and proud of themselves.

Question 4

What does great customer experience mean in your business?

Great customer experience means my clients feel completely at ease and supported throughout the entire process. It's about removing pressure and guiding them gently so they can enjoy the session. When clients walk in feeling unsure and leave feeling confident and surprised by themselves, that's success. Seeing themselves positively in their images is the most rewarding outcome.

Question 5

What impact do you want your business to have?

I want to help women truly see and believe in their own beauty, one person at a time. There is a powerful moment when a client recognises themselves in a new way, and that transformation is everything to me. These photographs become lasting memories for their loved ones, preserving connection and identity. If I can help women feel proud to exist in those memories, I know I'm making a difference.
