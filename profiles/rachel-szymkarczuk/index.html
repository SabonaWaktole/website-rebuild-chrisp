Rachel Szymkarczuk

Founder

HitLocal Hub

Technology & Digital / Professional Services

Overview

Rachel Szymkarczuk is the founder of HitLocal Hub, a digital platform focused on helping local businesses increase visibility, generate leads, and strengthen community engagement. She is passionate about creating sustainable solutions that empower businesses beyond traditional social media channels.

Background

Rachel Szymkarczuk is an entrepreneur and digital strategist with expertise in platform development, digital marketing, and local economic growth. She founded HitLocal Hub to address the growing challenges local businesses face in achieving sustainable visibility and engagement in an increasingly algorithm-driven digital landscape. Under her leadership, the platform has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem that combines business promotion, lead generation, marketplace tools, and customer engagement features. Rachel has developed scalable monetisation models including subscription offerings, referral programs, and advertising solutions to support long-term revenue growth. Her strategic vision centres on empowering businesses with greater control over their marketing while strengthening local economies through improved community connections. She continues to drive the platform’s expansion with a focus on innovation, accessibility, and measurable business outcomes.

Interview

Question 1

What inspired you to start HitLocal Hub?

HitLocal Hub was inspired by the growing challenge local businesses face in gaining consistent visibility and engagement. Many businesses rely heavily on social media platforms, where reach is unpredictable and controlled by algorithms. I saw an opportunity to create a platform that gives businesses more control, while also making it easier for communities to discover and support local. The goal was to build something that delivers real value—not just exposure, but connection and growth.

Question 2

What problem were you determined to solve?

The key problem was the disconnect between local businesses and their communities, combined with limited visibility and fragmented marketing channels. Businesses often struggle to reach the right audience consistently, while consumers lack a central place to easily discover and engage with local services. HitLocal Hub was created to solve both sides by bringing everything together into one streamlined, accessible platform.

Question 3

What differentiates HitLocal Hub from other platforms?

What differentiates HitLocal Hub is that it is more than a directory or advertising platform—it is a complete ecosystem. It combines business promotion, lead generation, marketplace functionality, and community engagement in one place. Most importantly, it moves beyond algorithm-driven visibility and provides businesses with a more stable, controlled way to connect with their audience.

Question 4

What impact do you want HitLocal Hub to have?

The impact is centred around strengthening local economies and creating more connected, thriving communities. By making it easier for people to support local businesses, we can help keep spending within local areas, increase opportunities for business growth, and build stronger community relationships. This reflects the broader vision of becoming the leading local platform where businesses grow, communities connect, and local economies thrive.

Question 5

What’s next for HitLocal Hub?

The next phase is focused on scaling the platform across multiple regions while maintaining strong local relevance. This includes expanding business adoption, increasing member engagement, and continuing to evolve the platform with features that deliver greater value. The long-term vision is to become the go-to local platform where businesses grow and communities connect.
