Linda Dunkin

Owner & Fractional Marketing Manager

Market My Brand

Professional Services

Overview

Strategic marketing leader with 20+ years of experience helping businesses grow through tailored marketing strategies and hands-on leadership.

Background

Linda Dunkin is the Owner and Director of Market My Brand, a consultancy built to give entrepreneurs access to high-level marketing expertise without the need for a full-time hire. With over two decades of experience, she has worked across marketing strategy, campaign execution, and brand leadership for a diverse range of businesses. Linda operates as a Fractional Marketing Manager, guiding business owners through complex marketing challenges with clarity and precision. She is known for her 'marketing GP' approach�diagnosing gaps, solving immediate issues, and engaging specialists when required. Her work is deeply relationship-driven, focusing on long-term partnerships rather than transactional engagements. Linda's success is reflected in the trust she builds and the sustained growth of her clients.

Interview

Question 1

What inspired you to start this business?

I love working with entrepreneurial people who have vision, ambition, and the self-awareness to know when they need help. Whether a business is dealing with a lack of time, knowledge, or experience, nothing gives me more satisfaction than solving a problem and seeing the impact it has on their success. Starting Market My Brand allowed me to do this at scale while forming meaningful partnerships.

Question 2

What problem were you determined to solve?

Marketing has evolved significantly, and what one person could manage years ago is no longer realistic. There are now countless channels, tools, and specialisations to navigate. I position myself as a marketing GP�identifying gaps, addressing what we can internally, and bringing in the right specialists or technologies when needed. This ensures businesses get the right support without overwhelm.

Question 3

What differentiates your business?

My work is never transactional. I immerse myself deeply in each client's business, building genuine trust and understanding what makes their brand unique. This long-term partnership approach allows me to deliver strategies that are not only effective but also sustainable over time.

Question 4

What metrics matter most to you?

Retention is the most important metric for me. Long-term relationships with clients, colleagues, and suppliers indicate that the work we are doing is meaningful and effective. While I enjoy solving specific problems quickly, the real measure of success is the longevity and strength of the partnerships I build.

Question 5

Who is your ideal client?

My ideal clients are business owners who are looking for someone they can truly trust to champion their brand. They value strategic thinking, are open to guidance, and understand the importance of investing in the right marketing support to achieve sustainable growth.
