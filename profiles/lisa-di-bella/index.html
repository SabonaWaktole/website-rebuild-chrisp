Lisa Di Bella

Owner

Di Bella Cleaning Services

Professional Services / Property

Overview

Owner and Manager of Di Bella Cleaning Services, overseeing staff, scheduling, client relationships, quality assurance, and daily operations while ensuring high standards, team performance, and exceptional service delivery.

Background

With over 10 years of experience, I am the Owner and Manager of Di Bella Cleaning Services, leading a growing team delivering high-quality residential and commercial cleaning solutions. I have built the business on strong values of reliability, care, and attention to detail, earning industry recognition and client trust. My role involves overseeing daily operations, staff coordination, scheduling, and maintaining consistent quality standards across all services. I work closely with both clients and team members to ensure expectations are met, issues are resolved efficiently, and a high level of service is always delivered. I am passionate about developing my team through training, clear communication, and ongoing support, while continuously improving systems and processes to strengthen business performance and client satisfaction.

Interview

Question 1

What inspired you to start this business?

I was inspired to start Di Bella Cleaning Services from a genuine passion for helping others and making a difference in people’s everyday lives. I wanted to create a service that people could truly rely on—one that goes beyond cleaning to provide care, trust, and peace of mind in their homes.

What problem were you determined to solve?

I was determined to solve the lack of consistency, reliability, and attention to detail often found in the cleaning industry.
